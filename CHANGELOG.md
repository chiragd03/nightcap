# Changelog

Each part of Nightcap is released on its own, tagged by part and version.

## 1.0.0 (November 2026)
- **Setup** (`setup-v1.0.0`): the `nightcap` command. Guided setup on this computer (Docker), Kubernetes, Google Cloud or another machine, and a dashboard to manage servers.
- **Server** (1.0): households, profiles, Homes with picks, Smart Search, Live TV, Play on TV, the web app.
- **Android TV** (`tv-v1.0.0`): the TV app for Android TV and Google TV.
- **Samsung** (`samsung-v1.0.0`) and **LG** (`lg-v1.0.0`): the TV apps for Samsung (Tizen) and LG (webOS).
